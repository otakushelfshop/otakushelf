Sizes, scales & pricing

Sizes depend on the sculpt. Some figures follow standard scales such as 1/16 or 1/8, and some can be resized to fit a particular display space. Larger figures and complex bases take more work, so prices vary from piece to piece and depending on scale.

Current prices are on the price list. Exact measurements are listed with each piece in the gallery and if you need a figure at a particular size, just ask.

Caring for your figure

  • Dusting: use a soft brush or a gentle puff of air.
  • Display: keep figures out of direct sunlight and away from high heat.
  • Cleaning: avoid harsh cleaners, and never soak painted surfaces.
  • Handling: pick figures up by the base or another solid part and never by delicate pieces like hair, weapons, or accessories.
  • Storage: wrap the figure and support any fragile parts carefully.
  • Repairs: if something chips or breaks, send us a photo and we will see whether a repair is possible and can help advise you.

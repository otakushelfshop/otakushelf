All purchases go through our Etsy shop, which handles payment securely. Here’s how it works:

Shipping

  • Where: we ship within Canada and internationally.
  • When: most orders ship about two weeks after you order. Larger or more detailed pieces can take longer.
  • Cost: shipping is calculated by Etsy at checkout. Very large or unusually shaped pieces may need a separate quote, so please ask first.
  • Customs: orders shipped outside Canada may be charged customs duties or fees, which are the buyer’s responsibility.
  • Address: please double-check your shipping address on Etsy before ordering.

Sizes, scales & pricing

Sizes depend on the sculpt. Many figures follow standard scales such as 1/16 or 1/8, and some can be resized to fit a particular display space. Larger figures and complex bases take more work, so prices vary by piece and by scale.

Current prices are on the prices page, and exact measurements are listed with each piece in the gallery. If you need a figure at a particular size, just ask.

Caring for your figure

  • Dusting: use a soft brush or a gentle puff of air.
  • Display: keep figures out of direct sunlight and away from high heat.
  • Cleaning: avoid harsh cleaners, and never soak painted surfaces.
  • Handling: pick figures up by the base or another solid part, never by delicate pieces like hair, weapons, or accessories.
  • Storage: wrap the figure carefully and support any fragile parts.
  • Repairs: if something chips or breaks, send us a photo and we’ll let you know whether a repair is possible.

About Otaku Shelf

We are a small Canadian-based studio that turns digital sculptures into hand-painted collectibles you will be proud to add to your collection.

Our Origin

What had originally begun as our own passion for collecting, creating, and bringing beloved pop-culture characters to life in our collection has grown into a creative business that focuses on making unique display pieces for fellow fans and fellow collectors. We are the biggest fans of the sculptures we make, so turning a great digital sculpture into something you can actually put on your shelf never gets old to us.

Our work and creation philosophy is simple:

We build collectibles that should be more than something you own. They should be something you are proud to display on your shelf.

What We Make

We mostly make anime, and video-game figures, along with fantasy, sci-fi, and other pop-culture pieces. Whether you are looking for an iconic anime character, a favourite video-game hero, or villain, or a striking centrepiece for your collection, our goal is to make that character as realistic and appealing as possible.

We care about clean prints, smooth finishes, expressive paintwork, and figures that look great on display. We are open to custom ideas, and many pieces can be made in different sizes depending on the model. For more information about custom work, please see Custom Work.

We are also happy to discuss R-rated and Mature themes, we evaluate each case by case and have zero tolerance for hateful, racist, sexist, or any other form of discriminatory content.

How a Figure is Made

Every figure is a labour of detail and intention. We combine modern resin and filament 3D printing with traditional model-making and airbrushing techniques, and each piece gets individual attention from start to finish. Following these steps below to create a figure ensures that the final product is something you will be proud to display on your shelf.

  1. Planning, Preparation, and Design

    In this beginning stage, we choose the sculpture and work out the size and how to split the figure into parts to print, fixing 3D model files.

  2. Printing

    Next, we prepare the files, supports, and print settings, to assure that the end result is consistent and high-quality. Each part is printed in resin or filament depending on the design.

  3. Cleaning Up

    To clean up any defects, resin parts are washed and cured. Any filament and resin supports are removed, and everything is put through quality control. After assuring each piece is perfect, the surfaces are tidied.

  4. Prep

    From here, all of the figures parts are sanded, filled, and primed until they are ready for paint. This is a crucial step to ensure that the paint adheres well and that the final result is smooth and clean.

  5. Painting

    Painting starts with a clean, primed surface. We build up the main colours with the airbrush, then work in shadows, highlights, textures, and small details with brushes. Depending on the figure, we finish with matte, satin, or gloss effects wherever they suit the piece.

  6. Finish

    To assure the long-term durability of the figure, we apply the right finish, assemble the figure, inspect it, and pack it carefully for shipping.

On average, most orders take about two weeks from start to finish; larger or more detailed projects can take longer. Shipping times vary depending on the destination, and we will provide an estimated delivery date when you place your order.

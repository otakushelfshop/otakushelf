About Otaku Shelf

Our story

Otaku Shelf began with our own passion for collecting, creating, and bringing beloved pop-culture characters to life. It has since grown into a creative business that makes unique display pieces for fellow fans and collectors. We’re the biggest fans of the sculptures we make, and turning a great digital sculpture into something you can put on your shelf never gets old.

Our philosophy is simple:

A collectible should be more than something you own. It should be something you’re proud to display.

What we make

We mostly make anime and video-game figures, along with fantasy, sci-fi, and other pop-culture pieces. Whether you’re looking for an iconic anime character, a favourite video-game hero or villain, or a striking centrepiece for your collection, our goal is to bring that character to life as faithfully and beautifully as possible.

We care about clean prints, smooth finishes, expressive paintwork, and figures that look great on display. Many pieces can be made in different sizes, and we’re always open to custom ideas. See custom work for details, and our policies for what we will and won’t make.

How a figure is made

Every figure is a labour of detail and intention. We combine modern resin and filament 3D printing with traditional model-making and airbrushing techniques, and each piece gets individual attention from start to finish.

  1. Planning and design

    We choose the sculpture, decide on the size, repair the 3D model files where needed, and work out how to split the figure into parts for printing.

  2. Printing

    We set up the files, supports, and print settings for consistent, high-quality results. Each part is printed in resin or filament, depending on the design.

  3. Clean-up

    Resin parts are washed and cured, supports are removed, and every part is checked for defects before its surfaces are tidied up.

  4. Prep

    All of the figure’s parts are sanded, filled, and primed. This crucial step helps the paint adhere well and keeps the final finish smooth and clean.

  5. Painting

    We build up the main colours with an airbrush, then add shadows, highlights, textures, and fine details by hand with brushes.

  6. Finishing and packing

    A protective matte, satin, or gloss coat is applied to suit the piece. Then we assemble the figure, inspect it, and pack it carefully for shipping.

Most orders take about two weeks from start to finish; larger or more detailed projects can take longer. Delivery times depend on the destination, and we’ll give you an estimated delivery date when you order.

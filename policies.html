Overview

Otaku Shelf is a small Canadian studio that 3D-prints and hand-paints collectibles to order. Every purchase is made through our Etsy shop, so by placing an order you agree to these policies, our terms & conditions, and Etsy’s own terms and policies.

What We Are Happy to Make

Yes, gladly

  • Anime and video-game characters
  • Fantasy, sci-fi, and other pop-culture figures
  • Statues, dioramas, and display pieces
  • Original and fan-inspired designs
  • Hobby accessories, like paint racks
  • Custom sizes, where the sculpt allows

Case by case

  • Mature themes
  • Violent, gory, or horror themes
  • Very large or unusually complex pieces
  • Customer-supplied files or heavy modifications

For anything in the “case by case” column, please contact us before ordering so we can confirm whether it’s something we can take on.

What We Would Not Make

We do not print, paint, or sell:

  • Hateful or discriminatory content, including anything that attacks or demeans people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar characteristics.
  • Sexual or sexually suggestive content that depictions in any manner a minor or person who is underage, or even a fictional/humanoid/alien character who is, or appears to be, underaged or a minor.
  • Pieces that harass, threaten, or sexualize real people, governments, religions, or organizations, or that impersonate or depict a real person without their consent.
  • Functional weapons, weapon parts, or anything designed to be mistaken for a real weapon.
  • Files you do not have the right to have printed, such as sculptures that are copyrighted or trademarked, or that are not licensed for personal use only.
  • Anything illegal in Canada or in the country its being shipped to.

Our Right to Refuse Service

We reserve the right to decline or cancel any order or request at our discretion. That includes, for example, requests that conflict with the policies above, projects we cannot produce to our standards, and customers who are abusive or disrespectful.

If we cancel an order you have already paid for, you will receive a full refund through Etsy.

Ordering & Payment

  • All orders and payments go through our storefrontEtsy shop. This website is a showcase only; nothing is sold or paid for here.
  • We will never ask you to pay outside Etsy (for example, by e-transfer or a payment link sent by message).
  • Prices listed are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and do not include shipping. Any applicable taxes are calculated by Etsy at checkout.
  • Etsy’s Terms of Use and buyer policies apply to every transaction. Questions about payments, charges, or refunds handled by Etsy are covered by Etsy’s help centre.

Custom Orders

Please contact us before ordering anything custom, such as a different size, a character not on our price list, or changes to an existing design.

We will confirm what is possible, along with the price and timeline, before you pay. Accepted custom work is then ordered through a listing on our Etsy shop, and the same policies apply.

Processing & Shipping

  • Every piece is made to order. Most orders take about two weeks to complete before they ship; larger or more detailed projects can take longer.
  • Please double-check your shipping address on Etsy. We cannot be responsible for orders sent to an incorrect address.
  • Orders shipped to the USA, or internationally, may be subject to customs duties or fees, which are the buyer is responsibility.

Cancellations & Returns

Because every piece is made to order, all sales are final once work on your order has started. We can’t accept returns or exchanges for change of mind.

If you need to cancel, message us on Etsy as soon as possible. If we have not started on your piece yet, we will do our best to help.

Damage, Repairs & Variations

Every piece is inspected and packed carefully before it ships. If something arrives damaged, or chips or breaks later, send us a few photos and we’ll see what might be possible for a repair.

Each figure is painted by hand, so small differences in colour and detail from the photos are normal, and part of what makes every piece one of a kind.

Customer Service

The best way to reach us about an order is through Etsy messages, so everything stays together with your purchase. You can also find us through the links on our contact section.

We’re a small studio and reply as quickly as we can. We ask that all messages stay respectful; we may stop responding to, or decline orders from, anyone who is abusive toward us.

Fan Art & Trademarks

Many of our pieces are fan-made tributes inspired by existing characters. Otaku Shelf is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators, studios, or publishers of those works. All characters, names, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Sculptors and artists are credited with each piece wherever we know them.

Privacy

Payments are processed by Etsy; we never see your card details. We only use the name and address Etsy shares with us to make and ship your order. We may share photos of finished pieces on this site and our social media; just let us know if you would rather we did not show yours.

Changes to these policies

We may update these policies from time to time, and the latest version will always be on this page. Your order is covered by the policies in place when you bought it.
